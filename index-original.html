About Our Studio

关于我们

Horizon is a bilingual creative studio founded in 2020, dedicated to bridging cultural gaps through design and technology. Our team of designers, developers, and strategists work across time zones to deliver projects that resonate with both Eastern and Western sensibilities.

We believe that great design transcends language barriers, creating intuitive experiences that feel native to every user. Our process combines meticulous attention to detail with bold creative vision.
